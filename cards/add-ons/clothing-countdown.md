### 👔 Clothing Countdown Add-On  
**Strip schedule — one piece every 10 minutes**  

Before each new dare, one or both persons must remove a piece of clothing — no exceptions, no delays.

- Every 10 minutes = one item removed
- Decide the order beforehand or let them choose
- No putting clothes back on until the summit

> *"Hope you dressed in layers — you're going to need them!"*
