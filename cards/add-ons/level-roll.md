## 🎲 Post-Hike Level Roll

At the end of each Kinktrails hike, each player rolls one die to determine their personal level for the next hike.  
    Your next level is based on the highest level reached this hike +1.
    The die roll decides how far you drop or rise within the new limit.

🧮 How it works:

Let’s say the highest level reached this hike was Level 2. That means for the next hike:  
Players roll for a random level up to Level 3.  
- Roll 1–2: Level 1
- Roll 3–4: Level 2
- Roll 5–6: Level 3
