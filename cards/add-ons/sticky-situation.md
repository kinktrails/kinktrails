### 🍯 Sticky Situation Add-On  
**No cleaning until the summit — embrace the mess**  

Once any sticky, messy, or gooey substance touches your body, no cleaning up is allowed until you reach the summit.

- Chocolate, syrup, fruit juice — it all stays put
- Let the mess build up throughout the hike
- Water is only allowed for drinking, not cleaning

> *"You're going to be deliciously messy by the end of this..."*
