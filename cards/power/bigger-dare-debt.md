### 💳 Bigger Dare Debt
**Skip up to 3 dares — debt compounds weekly.**

Play this card to skip 1-3 dares immediately. Your partner receives **1 Favor Token per skipped dare**.

**Compounding Interest:**
- Every week they hold tokens, they gain **1 additional token**
- Example: Skip 2 dares = 2 tokens. After 2 weeks = 4 tokens total

Partner can cash in tokens anytime:
- 1 token = simple dare from unlocked level
- 3 tokens = simple dare from any level
- 5 tokens = weekend control
- 10 tokens = full week control
- 20 tokens = month of unlimited requests
- 50 tokens = 10-hike series at Level 5 (one per week for 10 weeks)

After use, **pass this card to your partner** (but they keep the tokens until cashed in).

> *"Skip now, watch the debt grow... exponentially."*
