### 🌪️ Power Vacuum  
**Chaos time — shuffle and redistribute all power cards!**

Collect ALL power cards from both players, shuffle them completely, then deal them out randomly again.

Everyone gets the same number of cards they had before, but now it's a surprise what you're holding!

This card gets shuffled back in too — ultimate chaos!
