### 📍 Location Choice
**You pick exactly where they perform their next dare.**

Play this card after a dare is revealed but before it's performed.

- Creek crossing (wet and messy)
- Main trail (risky but thrilling)
- Deep in the bush (maximum privacy)
- Anywhere else you think would be interesting

Partner must perform the dare at your chosen spot.

After use, **pass this card to your partner**.
