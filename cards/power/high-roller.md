### 🎲 High Roller  
**Turn any single dice roll into a double-dice combo instantly!**

Play immediately after a single die is rolled — that number becomes a double (1 becomes 1-1, 6 becomes 6-6, etc.).

Your partner must now complete the intense variant instead of the normal dare.

After use, pass this card to your partner.

> *"One die? Let's make this interesting..."*
