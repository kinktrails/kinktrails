### 🎫 Dare Debt
**Skip this dare — owe a favor later.**

Play this card to skip the current dare immediately.

Your partner gets a **Favor Token** to cash in whenever they want — later this hike, next hike, or days/weeks later.

- They choose what you do when cashing it in
- Must be from unlocked levels (or one higher)
- No advance warning when they'll use it

After use, **pass this card to your partner**.

> *"Skip now, pay later... with interest."*
